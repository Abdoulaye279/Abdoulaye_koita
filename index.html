Flagship research project

Next-Generation Black Phosphorene-Based 2D SERS Sensor for Ultrasensitive Ammonium Nitrate Detection

Co-author of a scientific paper on a surface-enhanced Raman spectroscopy (SERS) sensor built on a two-dimensional black phosphorene substrate, designed for the sensitive detection of ammonium nitrate, a direct concern for industrial and environmental safety.

The work covers characterization of the 2D material, analysis of the Raman signal enhancement mechanism, and physical interpretation of the experimental results, with a Results and Discussion section built from laboratory data.

Presented at the International Conference of Physics Students (ICPS) 2026 in Rabat, as both an oral presentation and a poster.

Predictive radiation protection study following NF C 15-160 and Moroccan decree n° 2.23.151, covering conventional radiology, mammography, CT scanning, and panoramic dental radiography: lead-shielding thickness calculations, zoning, cross-validation via Dosimex.
