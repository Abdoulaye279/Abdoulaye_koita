Research and Engineering

Academic and applied projects

Fundamental physics research, collaborative engineering projects, and impact-driven initiatives.

Theoretical physics

Theoretical inequalities for the 2D electron gas

Proof of the diamagnetic inequality and other theoretical bounds on the kinetic energy density of a uniform 2D electron gas under a perpendicular magnetic field, with supporting mathematical derivations.

Energy, impact

BioCycle Solution Africa: rural biodigesters

Founder of a small-scale methanization initiative: design of a biodigestion circuit (anaerobic digester, compressor, gas filtration, storage), experimental protocol for evaluation, and impact analysis.

Nanotechnology

Quantum dots: theory and applications

Study of the electronic and optical properties of quantum dots: quantum confinement, applications in optoelectronics (LEDs, solar cells), and biomedical imaging. Group project.

Internship, Iso Qual Tech

Radiation protection study across four medical facilities

Predictive radiation protection study following NF C 15-160 and Moroccan decree n° 2.23.151, covering conventional radiology, mammography, CT scanning, and panoramic dental imaging.

Embedded systems

Smart proximity radar

Design and implementation of an obstacle-detection radar on an Arduino UNO with an HC-SR04 sensor, with LED and buzzer user feedback. Group project.

Signal processing

Quantization noise of an analog-to-digital converter

Simulation model of quantization noise, analyzing its effect on signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) and effective number of bits (ENOB) across different input signals and quantization levels.
