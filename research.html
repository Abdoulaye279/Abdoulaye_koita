FLAGSHIP RESEARCH PROJECT

Black Phosphorene-Based 2D SERS Sensor for Ultrasensitive Ammonium Nitrate Detection

Co-author of a scientific paper on a surface-enhanced Raman spectroscopy (SERS) sensor built on a two-dimensional black phosphorene substrate, designed for the sensitive detection of ammonium nitrate.

The work covers characterization of the 2D material, analysis of the Raman signal enhancement mechanism, and physical interpretation of the experimental results, with a Results and Discussion section.

Presented at the International Conference of Physics Students (ICPS) 2026 in Rabat, as both an oral presentation and a poster.

Publications
Abdoulaye Koita, Ferdinand Nouaye*, and Abdelouahed El Fatimy*, "Black Phosphorene-Based 2D SERS Sensor for Ultrasensitive Ammonium Nitrate Detection."
Ferdinand Nouaye*, Abdoulaye Koita, Rajesh Desapogu, Jean Decker, Abdelmajid Ait Abdelkader, Petr Neugebauer, Rachid El Fatimy, and Abdelouahed El Fatimy*, "SERS-Based Fertilizer Detection."
